# How to use this toolkit

## What this toolkit is

A set of plain text files you read in order, plus a set of prompts you copy into an AI chat tool.

It is not software. There is nothing to install. Every file opens in any text editor, on any phone, or in Google Docs.

## How to use the AI prompts

The prompts in `02-branding-and-marketing/` are written to be copied and pasted into an AI chat tool. They work in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Microsoft Copilot. The free versions are fine.

### The rules

**One prompt at a time.** Paste it. Wait for the answer. Do not paste the next prompt until you are happy with the current one.

**Never accept the first answer.** Every branding prompt gives you 5 options. Read all 5. Pick the closest one even if it is not right. Then use the refine prompt to ask for 5 new versions that keep what you liked and fix what you did not. Do this two or three times. The third round is usually much better than the first.

**Start a new chat for each big task.** A chat that runs too long makes the AI forget earlier details. Start fresh for naming, then fresh again for social content.

**Keep your answers.** Save the brand brief, the name, the colours and the voice guide in one document. Later prompts ask you to paste them in.

**Fill in every [SQUARE BRACKET].** The prompts have placeholders. If you leave them, the AI invents answers and the output is generic.

**The AI will be wrong about South African law.** It is a writing helper, not a lawyer or an accountant. Never let it tell you what a fee is, what a form is called, or what a threshold is. Check that in [Sources and verification register](../05%20Look%20it%20up/03-sources-and-verification-register.md) and on the official site.

### The refine prompt

Use this after every step in [Branding prompts](../02%20Branding%20and%20marketing/01-branding-prompts.md):

```
I like option [NUMBER] best.

What works: [say what you like, in your own words]
What does not work: [say what is wrong]

Give me 5 new options that keep what works and fix what does not.
Do not repeat the options you already gave me.
Number them 1 to 5. After the 5 options, stop and ask me again.
```

### If the AI runs ahead

Some AI tools will answer three steps at once. When that happens, say:

```
Stop. Go back to step [X] only. Give me 5 options for that step and
nothing else. Wait for my answer before moving on.
```

## How the legal parts are written

Some paragraphs in this toolkit stay close to the wording of a law, a regulator, or another source listed in [Sources and verification register](../05%20Look%20it%20up/03-sources-and-verification-register.md). They are kept close to the source on purpose, so you can check them. They can be hard to read.

Every hard one is followed by a line that starts like this:

> **In plain words:**...

That line says the same thing in everyday English. If the legal sentence confuses you, skip it and read the plain line. You lose nothing.

Each heavy file also opens with a short table called "Words used in this file". Read that first. Every abbreviation in the toolkit is also explained in [Glossary](../05%20Look%20it%20up/01-glossary.md).

## How to read the facts in this toolkit

Anything with a rand figure, a percentage, a date, a form number or a section of an Act came from a source. All of those sources are listed in [Sources and verification register](../05%20Look%20it%20up/03-sources-and-verification-register.md) with a link.

Sources are marked **[Official]** when they come from government or a regulator. Those are the ones to trust. Everything else is a pointer.

### Before you act on a number

Open [Sources and verification register](../05%20Look%20it%20up/03-sources-and-verification-register.md), find the topic, click the official link, and confirm the figure. This takes two minutes and it is worth it, because:

- Tax thresholds change in February and take effect in March or April
- CIPC and licensing fees change without announcement
- Municipal rules differ between every municipality in the country
- Provincial fees differ between all nine provinces

If a figure in this toolkit disagrees with the official site, the official site is right.

## What this toolkit deliberately leaves out

Employment. The moment you hire someone, the Basic Conditions of Employment Act, PAYE, UIF and COIDA all apply, and mistakes there are expensive. Get proper advice before your first hire.

Raising money. Investors, loans, and grants each have their own rules. Nothing here covers them.

Anything above roughly R2 million turnover. At that point VAT, payroll and formal accounting change the picture, and you need a real accountant rather than a toolkit.

Trusts, partnerships and non-profits. This toolkit covers sole proprietors and private companies only.

Companies with more than one shareholder. The two Pty Ltd files assume you are the sole shareholder and the sole director. With a co-owner you also need a shareholders agreement, and your financial reporting obligation may change, because the owner-managed exemption depends on every shareholder also being a director.

Pricing. How much to charge is the hardest question a new business faces, and nothing here answers it. The toolkit tells you what a quote must contain, not what number to put on it.

Cash flow forecasting. The cash book in [Which template to use when](../03%20Paperwork%20and%20templates/01-which-template-to-use-when.md) records what already happened. It does not predict whether you can pay next month's bills.

Insurance detail. Each business type file says what cover to ask for. None of them compares products or prices. Use a broker.

## Common mistakes this toolkit is trying to prevent

**Spending money before earning any.** The most common failure. R15,000 on registration, logo and website before a single sale.

**Trading before a required licence is issued.** You cannot fix this afterwards.

**Registering a company you cannot afford to keep compliant.** R175 to register, far more each year to maintain.

**Filing nothing because you earned nothing.** Penalties grow monthly on returns you never submitted.

**Charging VAT without being registered.** That is fraud, not an administrative error.

**Mixing business and personal money.** It makes every other problem harder to fix.

**Using a "voetstoots" or "no refunds" clause.** For most consumer sales in South Africa, it does not work.

## Making the files easier to use

These are markdown files (`.md`). They open as plain text anywhere, but they look better in a markdown reader.

On a phone: open them in Google Drive, or any free markdown app.

On a computer: open them in any text editor, or upload them to Google Drive and open with a markdown viewer.

To make one printable document: open the file, copy the text, paste it into Google Docs or LibreOffice Writer, then export as PDF.

To edit them for your own use: change anything you like. They are yours now. If you give the edited version to someone else, keep [How this was made and how to check it](02-how-this-was-made.md) with it.

A free toolkit for one person starting and running a business in South Africa. You are the owner. You are also the only worker.

## The short version of the whole toolkit

You do not need most of what people tell you to get.

You can legally start selling today, as yourself, with no company registration, no logo, and no website. Earn money first. Add the formal parts as the business grows.

The four things that actually matter early:

1. A separate bank account for business money
2. Filing with SARS, even at zero income
3. A licence, if your business type needs one, before you trade
4. Records kept for five years

Three numbers changed in 2026 and most websites still have them wrong:

- Compulsory VAT registration: **R2.3 million**, not R1 million, from 1 April 2026
- Voluntary VAT registration: **R120,000**, not R50,000
- Turnover tax: **R2.3 million** threshold, with **0% on the first R600,000**

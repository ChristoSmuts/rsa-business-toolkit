## Part A: everyone, in order

**Before you trade**

- [ ] Sold something once and got paid, to prove people want it
- [ ] Opened a separate bank account for business money only, and asked the bank my instant payment limits
- [ ] If working from home: checked zoning, title deed and complex rules; told my household insurer
- [ ] Location switched off in my phone camera before photographing stock at home
- [ ] Checked whether my business type needs a licence (`01-core/04` and `04-business-types/`)
- [ ] Applied for that licence, if needed, and waited for it to be issued
- [ ] Checked zoning, if I work from home or from fixed premises

**Brand and documents**

- [ ] Chosen a name, and checked it on CIPC, as a.co.za domain, and on social media
- [ ] Bought the domain, even if there is no website yet
- [ ] Built a brand one-pager (`02-branding-and-marketing/01`)
- [ ] Built a quote template
- [ ] Built an invoice template, with correct VAT wording for my situation
- [ ] Written terms and conditions
- [ ] Written a privacy notice
- [ ] Set up a cash book spreadsheet
- [ ] Set up folders in Drive with a consistent naming system (`03-documents/02`)

**Compliance**

- [ ] Registered my Information Officer at inforegulator.org.za (free)
- [ ] Confirmed I have a SARS income tax number
- [ ] Registered as a provisional taxpayer
- [ ] Decided sole proprietor or company, and registered if needed
- [ ] If a company: confirmed the SARS registered representative
- [ ] If a company: filed beneficial ownership information (free)
- [ ] Got a B-BBEE affidavit signed, if a customer has asked (free)

**Marketing**

- [ ] Claimed and completed my Google Business Profile
- [ ] Set up WhatsApp Business with a catalogue and quick replies
- [ ] Asked my first three customers for reviews

**Every sale where I never meet the customer, or meet them once**
- [ ] Money reflected in my balance, not "pending", before anything is handed over
- [ ] No goods released on a screenshot, SMS, or PDF proof of payment
- [ ] Meeting in a public, busy, daylight place with cameras; someone told; someone with me
- [ ] For a vehicle: licence and ID copy received first, keys kept in my hand, no solo test drive

**Money hygiene**

- [ ] Photographing every slip on the day I get it
- [ ] Reconciling the cash book against the bank statement monthly
- [ ] Invoice numbers running in order with no gaps
- [ ] Recording enquiries and sales each month, not likes and followers

## Part D: the four things that cause real trouble

In order of how often they catch small South African businesses:

1. Not filing when you earn nothing. Penalties grow monthly on nil returns you never submitted. A dormant company is the worst version of this.

2. Trading before a required licence is issued. You cannot apply retrospectively, and you can be fined.

3. Charging VAT without being registered. This is fraud, not a mistake.

4. Mixing business and personal money. It makes every other problem on this list harder to fix.

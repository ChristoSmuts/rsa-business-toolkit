# Glossary

Short words, in plain words.

## Tax

**SARS** — South African Revenue Service. The tax authority. www.sars.gov.za

**VAT** — Value-Added Tax, 15%. Only charged by registered vendors.

**Turnover tax** — A simplified tax for micro businesses. 0% on the first R600,000.

**VAT264** — The form both buyer and seller sign so a notional input tax claim is valid.

**Tax invoice** — An invoice issued by a VAT vendor with specific required fields. Only vendors may issue one.

## Vehicles

**RWC / CoR** — Roadworthy Certificate, or Certificate of Roadworthiness.

## The short version

If your business turns over under R2.3 million a year, you do not have to register for VAT.

The voluntary registration threshold increased from R50,000 to R120,000 per year.

> **In plain words:** Below R2.3 million you choose. File the IRP6 by 31 August, even at 0%.

## What SARS wants from a sole proprietor

Business profit goes on your ITR12. See `03-documents/01` and section 56(2) of the Act.

## Your tax checklist

1. Register as a provisional taxpayer
2. If a company: file the ITR14 within 12 months
